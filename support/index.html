Support

For help with 07, email hello@triumlabs.io. Replies are sent by email.

Camera permission

07 needs Camera access to take a photograph. If you denied it, open iOS Settings, choose 07, and turn Camera on. Return to 07 and reopen the camera.

Photos permission

Save to Photos is optional and uses add-only access. If access is off, 07 keeps working and keeps finished photographs in its own private library. To enable it later, open iOS Settings, choose 07, allow Photos additions, then turn Save to Photos on in 07 Settings. Eligible local photographs are retried in the background.

Location permission

Location metadata is optional and off by default. Enable it in 07 Settings, then allow location access when iOS asks. If previously denied, change the permission in iOS Settings. Capture still works without location.

Your photos in 07, and deleting them

07 keeps your photographs in its own private library on your iPhone. Erase or Delete removes one photograph from 07; Format or Delete All clears everything in 07. Neither action deletes anything from your Photos library. Removing the app also removes 07's library, so confirm any important Photos copies first.

Complimentary photographs and Full 07 Camera

07 includes 36 complimentary photographs developed through its complete on-device image process. The one-time Full 07 Camera purchase unlocks unlimited 07-developed photographs. It is not a subscription. In 07, open Settings with the gear button (SET UP in version 1.0), then choose Unlock unlimited photos (Full card in version 1.0) to view the current localized price or purchase at any time.

Restore a purchase

In 07, open Settings with the gear button (SET UP in version 1.0) and choose Restore purchase. It is also on the Unlock unlimited photos screen. Use the Apple Account that originally purchased the non-consumable.

Report a problem

Email the iPhone model, iOS version, 07 version, what you expected, what happened, and the shortest steps that reproduce it. Do not attach private photographs unless they are necessary and you choose to share them.
