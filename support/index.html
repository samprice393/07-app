Support

For help with 07, email sam@triumleads.com. Replies are sent by email.

Camera permission

07 needs Camera access to take a photograph. If you denied it, open iOS Settings, choose 07, and turn Camera on. Return to 07 and reopen the camera.

Photos permission

Save to Photos is optional and uses add-only access. If access is off, Card 01 remains usable and keeps finished photographs locally. To enable it later, open iOS Settings, choose 07, allow Photos additions, then turn Save to Photos on in 07 Settings. Eligible local photographs are retried in the background.

Location permission

Location metadata is optional and off by default. Enable it in 07 Settings, then allow location access when iOS asks. If previously denied, change the permission in iOS Settings. Capture still works without location.

Card 01, ERASE, and FORMAT

Card 01 is 07's private local library. ERASE removes one Card 01 item; FORMAT clears Card 01. Neither action deletes Photos-library assets. Removing the app also removes Card 01, so confirm any important Photos copies first.

Complimentary photographs and Full 07 Camera

07 includes 36 complimentary photographs developed through its complete on-device image process. The one-time Full 07 Camera purchase unlocks unlimited 07-developed photographs. It is not a subscription. Open Camera → SET UP → Full card to view the current localized price or purchase before the complimentary card is full.

Restore a purchase

Open Camera → SET UP → Full card and choose Restore purchase. Restore is also available directly in Settings. Use the Apple Account that originally purchased the non-consumable.

Report a problem

Email the iPhone model, iOS version, 07 version, what you expected, what happened, and the shortest steps that reproduce it. Do not attach private photographs unless they are necessary and you choose to share them.
